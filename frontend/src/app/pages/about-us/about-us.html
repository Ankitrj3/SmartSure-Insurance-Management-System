OUR JOURNEY

Building Trust Through Innovation

Founded in 2015, SmartSure emerged from a simple observation: insurance was too complicated, too expensive, and too impersonal. Our founders, a team of insurance veterans and tech innovators, set out to change that.

What started as a small startup with a big vision has grown into a comprehensive insurance platform serving over 10,000 customers across the nation. We've maintained our commitment to transparency, affordability, and exceptional customer service while continuously innovating to meet the evolving needs of modern families and businesses.

Today, SmartSure stands at the intersection of traditional insurance expertise and modern technology, offering a seamless digital experience without sacrificing the personal touch that makes insurance truly valuable.
